01 · ABOUT

What is IPDB?

IPDB brings palaeoecological and palaeoenvironmental information into a shared digital space designed for research, learning and collaboration.

Its purpose is to make long-term records easier to discover and understand, supporting work on past ecosystems, climate variability, biodiversity, geology, archaeology and environmental change.
